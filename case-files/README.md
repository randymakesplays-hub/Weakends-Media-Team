## Reading the portfolio

**The ICP question this set answers.** Two of the eight accounts turned out not to be
marketing prospects at all. Pete and Ooh Wee are both word-of-mouth saturated and told
Randy so directly; Pete had a real constraint underneath it (manpower), Ooh Wee never
surfaced one. Aaron and Jose are supply, not demand. That leaves the genuinely
demand-constrained accounts — Chiquita, Flawless, Spotless — as the actual ICP, and they
share a profile: **a business that already has customers and a service worth selling, but
no working front end.**

**The disqualifying signal is cheap to detect.** "I'm doing good," "word of mouth is my best
advertisement," "I don't need any more customers" — all three surfaced in the first two
minutes of a call. That is a first-call qualifying question, not a discovery finding.

**The recurring gap.** Across Pete, Flawless, Jose, Aaron and Chiquita the same thing keeps
happening: real work is committed or delivered with **no agreed price, rate, or terms**. With
Chiquita a price existed and was withdrawn. The agency needs a default rate card and a
standard rev-share floor so terms are a quote, not an invention, on every call.

**What works.** The spec-build. Four weeks of silence from Chiquita, one unrequested
finished website, warm same-day reply. Worth making the standard second touch on any lead
that goes dark.
