## Diagnosis

**He is demand-constrained in the purest form in this book.** Every other account either
already has a customer flow (Pete, Ooh Wee) or has a business that predates the agency
(Flawless, Spotless). Johntes is standing up a solo operation from zero, and the window in
which that gets decided is short — a barber leaving a franchise is in a race to bring his
existing chair clients with him before they get reassigned to whoever replaces him.

**That makes the booking system the urgent deliverable, not the brand.** The single thing
that converts a franchise client into his client is a way to book him personally. A
rebrand can follow; a booking link cannot wait.

**The personal relationship cuts both ways.** It buys trust and speed that cold outreach
never gets — but it is also the condition under which terms most often go undefined, which
is already the recurring failure across this portfolio (see the index). A friend is the
*most* important person to put a written scope and price in front of, not the least.

---

## Risks

1. **No record of what was actually agreed.** The onboarding call is unrecorded and the
   relationship is informal. If the scope is only in two people's memories, there is
   nothing to point back to when it drifts.
2. **No terms.** Same gap as Pete, Flawless, Jose, Aaron and Chiquita. With a personal
   contact this is the likeliest of all of them to be left unaddressed indefinitely.
3. **Timing risk on the franchise exit.** Client portability is time-sensitive. If the
   startup package takes weeks, the clients that make his solo operation viable may
   already be gone.
4. **Everything below this line is inference.** His actual chair volume, pricing, current
   client count, location, and how he takes bookings today are all unknown and were never
   documented.
