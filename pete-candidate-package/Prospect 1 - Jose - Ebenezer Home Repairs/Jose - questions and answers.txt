QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
================================================================

Q.  How long have you been doing contracting work?
A.  10 years.

Q.  Only Texas, or other states too?
A.  Texas now. New Jersey and Florida before that.

Q.  Residential or commercial?
A.  Residential.

Q.  Any licenses or permits, or are you a subcontractor?
A.  "I'm a handyman." No license, no permits.

Q.  What kind of work do you do?
A.  Painting, tiling, sheetrock repair, installing doors, door
    hardware and locks, power gates and gate motors, garage door
    openers, plumbing repairs, light fixture hanging, and
    electrical repair - outlets, switches, lamps.

Q.  What job do you do most / are best at?
A.  Painting.

Q.  Do you work alone or have a team?
A.  "Me and my wife most of the time."

Q.  What areas do you cover?
A.  Katy, Cypress, Sugar Land, Houston, Pasadena, Spring, Conroe,
    and The Woodlands.

Q.  Have you worked on another team before?
A.  No. Solo company the whole 10 years.

Q.  How do you price a job?
A.  Has to see the work first - "I need to see the work so I can
    give the price." Prices labor only; customer buys material
    unless they ask for a full quote with materials included.

Q.  What do you charge the most for?
A.  Bathroom renovations - e.g. remove a bathtub, build a shower.
    $3,000 to $3,500 for labor.
    Tiling runs about $2.75 per square foot for labor.

Q.  What's your availability?
A.  Monday through Friday. Weekends are for family - emergencies
    only (his example: a broken faucet they can't shut off).

Q.  Anything you want to ask me?
A.  Asked how payment works - who gives the estimate, who passes
    it on, and how he gets paid.
