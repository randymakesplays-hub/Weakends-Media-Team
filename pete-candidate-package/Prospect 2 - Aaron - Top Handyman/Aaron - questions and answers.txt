QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
================================================================

Q.  How long have you been doing contracting work?
A.  Since 2009 - about 17 years. Didn't have a company at first.

Q.  Only Texas, or other states too?
A.  Has worked in Florida, Arkansas and Louisiana. Houston now.

Q.  Any parts of town you won't go to?
A.  No limits - fully mobile, will go anywhere in the Houston
    area. "As long as the dollar comes, doesn't really matter."

Q.  Any licensing or trades?
A.  Held an electrician's license but does not carry it now. Is
    working on getting it back.

Q.  What can you do, and what won't you do?
A.  Says there's nothing he can't do. Only limit: roof angles -
    he stays at 7-pitch and below, and has roofers for anything
    steeper.

Q.  What's your biggest strength / most experience?
A.  Flooring and demolition. Also says tile is his go-to - just
    finished two showers at one location.

Q.  Have you ever worked on a team?
A.  Yes. Prior military service. "There's nothing I haven't done
    or won't do."

Q.  Have you had anyone work under you?
A.  Yes - has used subcontractors on specific jobs, and has his
    own roofers.

Q.  What's your availability?
A.  Monday through Friday, plug and play, taking any and all
    work. Tries not to work Saturdays. Sundays reserved.

Q.  Do you have your own transportation?
A.  Yes.

Q.  What tools do you have?
A.  Everything - full tile setup, electrical tools, power tools,
    demo hammers. "There's not one thing that I actually need for
    any one job." Carries a trailer with all his tools in it.

Q.  How do you price jobs?
A.  Labor-only, on site, when the customer supplies all material:
    $400 A DAY. He does not run to the store or pick up
    materials - he shows up, does the work, that's it.

Q.  Residential and commercial?
A.  Both.

Q.  What's your competitive edge?
A.  "I'm fair in my pricing. I am meticulous in my work."
    And: "there's nothing that I wouldn't do to complete a job or
    make the customer happy... even if we as a team fail on a
    house, at least the customer is still happy with the product."

Q.  How old are you / are you from Texas?
A.  40. Born and raised in Texas, knows the area.

Q.  How many jobs a week could you take?
A.  Won't give a number - depends on the job. Wouldn't set
    expectations he can't meet: "that's not fair to me or fair to
    y'all." Works well off a punch list - used to do building
    maintenance, gets handed a list and knocks it all out in two
    or three days.

Q.  Any questions for me?
A.  None.
