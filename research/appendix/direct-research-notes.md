## Competitor scan — Charlotte market
Key strategic finding: MOST Charlotte competitors HIDE pricing (consultation/quote model):
- Gloss N Go Mobile Detailing (glossngoclt.com, @glossngoclt): no prices. Phone/email booking. Tagline "Where quality meets convenience." Serves Southpark, Ballantyne, Myers Park, etc.
- Caro Detail (carodetail.com): no prices; says "fair, transparent prices" without figures. Mobile + in-shop, Lake Norman area. Maintenance-plan upsell referenced.
- FC Auto Detailing (fc-cardetailing.com): consultation model; "interior-only starts ~$160", paint correction/ceramic "$1,000+". "175+ 5-star reviews" claim. Payments: cards/Venmo/Zelle/cash; same-day if space.
- Level Up Detailing (levelup-detailing.com): no prices; free-estimate form. Ceramic, tint, windshield protection. Detail = "4–5 hours".

Competitors that DO publish (price anchors):
- Prime Time Mobile Detailing (primetimemobiledetail.com) — BEST structural reference. 6 tiers × 3 vehicle sizes (Sedan / Non-Sedan / Third Row), each tier lists duration + inclusions + recommended frequency:
  - Light Detail $100/$120/$140 (1h15–2h, every 2–4 wks)
  - Big Time Detail $180/$220/$260 (2h15–2h45, every 6–12 mo, adds wax)
  - Show Time Detail $280/$320/$360 (full shampoo, single-stage polish, engine)
  - Prime Time Detail $380/$420/$460 (clay bar, 2-stage correction, headliner)
  - Platinum Detail $480/$520/$560 (3-stage correction, ceramic spray)
  - Diamond Detail $700/$900/$1,100 (9H ceramic, shop w/ pickup)
  - Promos: "20% off Flash Sale" w/ code, buy-now-pay-later financing.
- Oak Detailing: basic $150 sedan / $200 SUV-van; mid-tier $250 / $300.
- Panda Hub (marketplace): express from $79.91 sedan; full "Revive" from $227.10; "Black" w/ paint protection from $272.52.
- Charlotte Mobile Detail: mega-bundle $599 ("$916 value") — anchoring/strikethrough-value play.

Market takeaways:
- Realistic Charlotte mobile pricing bands: maintenance wash $100–150; standard full detail $180–280; deep/full $280–400; correction/ceramic $480–1,100+.
- Vehicle-size columns are the standard menu axis: Sedan / SUV(non-sedan) / Truck-3rd-row, +$20–60 per size step (or +$50–200 at premium tiers).
- Because most locals hide pricing, a CLEAN TRANSPARENT PRICE MENU is a genuine differentiator for Carolina Gloss — but client site currently says "custom quote," so creatives can use "starting at" pricing to stay compatible with the quote-first funnel.
- Common proof claims in market: review counts ("175+ 5-star"), 5.0 rating badges, before/afters.
