# Agent report — High-converting static ad creatives for mobile detailing (Meta)

## Executive summary
Statics drive ~60–70% of conversions on Meta accounts (Take Flight); static beats video for straightforward offers/limited-time promos/simple value props (Verde Media). Winning stack for a local detailer: **real before/after photography + price-anchored first-time offer + city callout + review/rating trust strip + one CTA**, as 4:5 feed + 9:16 story variants, pointed at a Meta lead form or a booking page whose price matches the ad. Economics: $20–50/day, ~$25–50 CPL auto-services benchmark (one detailing-adjacent campaign hit $14.96 CPL), leads in 7–14 days.

## 1. Formats ranked
Tier 1: (1) Before/after split static — "secret weapon" (ClicksGeek); name vehicle + effort in overlay ("2018 BMW 3-Series — 6-Hour Paint Correction", "5 Years of Swirls Removed"); dramatic subjects win (trashed interiors, swirled black paint); before = "that's disgusting", after = "that's amazing". (2) Offer-led static — "Interior Detail Special — First-Time Customers $99. Regularly $149. This Week Only: 10 Spots" (ClicksGeek); "$200 Off Ceramic This Month" → Meta Lead Form (Detailers Movement). (3) Review/social-proof static — stars + quote + before/after thumbnails; "4.9 Stars from 200+ Reviews"; specific testimonials beat "Great service!".
Tier 2: (4) Price-anchored package ads; 3-package carousel (3–5 cards best-converting, Verde). (5) "We come to you" convenience — supporting line, NOT the headline; one test cell only.
Foreplay static archetypes cross-check: Before/After, Us vs Them, Promotion/Discount, Features/Benefits.

## 2. Hooks
- Local callout: "Car owners in Charlotte —"
- Disgust interior: "When's the last time you looked under your kids' car seats?"
- Outcome over process: "Remove years of swirl marks and restore that showroom shine" (not "professional paint correction using advanced compounds"); recurring words: "showroom fresh", "mirror finish"
- Seasonal: "Protect your paint before winter salt damage"; NC spring = pollen
- Scarcity: "Only 3 spots left this week"
- Segment callout: "Tesla Owners: Protect Your Paint — Free Estimate"
- Mechanics: headline 5–7 words; primary text <125 chars before truncation; on statics visual hierarchy IS the hook.

## 3. Offers
- First-time offer on entry-level service = consensus cold-traffic converter; frame as welcoming, not desperation.
- Rule of 100: under $100 use %, over $100 use $ off. ($149 detail → "$50 off"; $79 express → "25% off")
- Always price-anchor: "Regularly $149 — First-Time $99"
- Free add-on (free interior wipe-down w/ exterior pkg) preserves price integrity; booking tools (OrbisX) support auto-upsells.
- Credible urgency: limited weekly slots ("10 spots this week") — natural for solo mobile operator.
- Seasonal calendar (Jobber): spring pollen/salt, summer wax/ceramic, fall leaf/sap, winter sealant/salt.
- "Free quote" CTA for variable-priced work (ceramic/correction).

## 4. Visual best practices
- Deliver 1080×1350 (4:5), 1080×1080 (1:1), 1080×1920 (9:16). 4:5 beats 1:1 in feed (up to +15% CTR, Billo).
- 9:16 safe zones: top ~14% and bottom ~20–35% clear.
- Text: 20% rule dead but heavy overlay still throttles delivery. One headline + one price/offer + one CTA on-image.
- One focal point, bold contrast, mobile-legible type (98% mobile). Carolina-blue/black/white color blocking = feed standout asset.
- Native vs designed: users scroll-skip ad-looking content; real job-site photos beat stock. Sweet spot = real driveway photo + clean designed overlay strip (not full poster, not raw lo-fi).
- Testing: 3–5 variants, one variable each, 7+ days, 25–50 events before judging; one new creative/week vs fatigue.

## 5. AI tells (cars are hardest subject)
- Warped/asymmetric badges (#1 tell), garbled plates, melted logos
- Mismatched spoke counts, ovalized rims, wrong caliper placement
- Broken reflections across panels (paint must mirror ONE coherent environment)
- Wrong repeating patterns in light internals
- Composite artifacts: edge halos, missing tire contact shadow ("floating car" — contact shadow is "the single most important thing", LotReady), wrong light direction, color-temp mismatch
- Trust: 90% want AI disclosure; 61% distrust advertising partly due to AI slop. Approach: real cars on real NC driveways (phone photos fine), design confined to overlay layer; AI only for background cleanup; never generate the car.

## 6. Social proof
- 88% trust Google reviews like personal recs; 97% read reviews; >4.5 stars lifts CTR; +1 star ≈ up to +44% conversion likelihood.
- 59% only trust rating backed by 20+ reviews → show count with stars: "4.9 · 200+ Google reviews".
- Counters: "500+ cars detailed". Testimonials: specific + name + neighborhood. City names on creative; 15-mile radius targeting norm.

## 7. Landing/booking
- Message match: ad offer/price must equal landing page. Sub-3s load, one headline/offer/CTA, 3–5 bullets, click-to-call, minimal-field calendar.
- Meta Lead Forms ~20–30% cheaper per lead but lower quality — needs fast follow-up (leads cold in hours).
- Detailer booking tools: Urable (the specialist), OrbisX, Automate, DetailBook, Detail Bookie, Mobile Tech RX (CRM/estimates). Generic (Calendly/Square/Acuity/Booksy) can't do vehicle-size pricing/service-area rules/deposits.
- If booking page shows package prices → "from $99" ads safe; quote-based → "free quote" CTA.

## 8. Benchmarks
- CPL: auto-sector ~$39 avg, range $25–50, 5–7% conv (admanage.ai); auto repair ~$28; all-industry $27.66 (WordStream 2025). Detailing case: 61 leads @ $14.96 CPL/30 days; tint shop 12→39 leads/mo, −46% CPL.
- Budget: $20–50/day start; min $20–30/day to learn; leads in 7–14 days; kill at 2× target CPL w/ no conversions; never double budget overnight.
- Max CPL = ticket × close rate (e.g. $150 detail × 30% close → target CPL < $20–30).

## Do / Don't
Do: real photos of dramatic transformations; 4:5+9:16+1:1 set; one offer/service/audience per campaign; price anchor + credible scarcity; stars WITH count; city names; instant lead follow-up; 3–5 variants, weekly refresh.
Don't: stock/generic clean-car shots; AI-generated cars; heavy on-image text; convenience as sole hook; generic testimonials; heavy discounting; broad targeting; complex forms; judging before 7 days/25–50 events.
