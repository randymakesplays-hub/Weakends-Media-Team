## 7. Creative hooks pulled straight from the call

- **"Clay bar" is the value word.** Dallas: *"you tell them you're going to clay bar their car — they're going to hear clay bar, like man that's something you gotta pay $300 for."* [@ 51:06](https://fathom.video/calls/810745895?timestamp=3066) Put "CLAY BAR INCLUDED" on the Pro/Deluxe creative — it reads expensive and costs no extra time.
- **Full detail, not a section.** Client's differentiator: *"I want to promote the full detail. Not just one section of the car."* Kill any "interior only / exterior only" positioning.
- **Ceramic soap + spray sealant** = the protection story for Deluxe → natural pollen-season campaign hook.
- **$10–20 to book** is a near-zero-friction CTA. "Book for $10 down" is a strong ad line.
