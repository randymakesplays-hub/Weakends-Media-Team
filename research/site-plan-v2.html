Updated with your intel: Weakends Agency, the "we fix weak ends" hook, the Clay-style 5-tab layout, "Book a performance trial" as the one CTA, light theme, no faces — all logos, reels, and links stay placeholders for now. This round is about two things: the words at the top and the order of the page.

01 · The brand in one line

Weakends fixes the weak ends

Your name carries the entire pitch — that's rare, and the site should ride it hard. The positioning line every page hangs off:

Positioning statement

Weakends is a media & AI agency for day-to-day business owners. We find your business's Weakends — content, ads, automation — and make them run strong all week.

Two rules for all copy on the site: every section either names a Weakend or proves we fix them — and the punchline never gets split. It's always Weakends (or a single Weakend), one word, capital W, never "weak ends."

02 · The top of the page

The money zone: nav → hero → proof strip

Visitors decide in the first screen. Clay's lesson: sentence one says what you are, sentence two names the dream, and proof appears before any selling paragraph. Here are three hero options — A is what's live in the prototype and my recommendation.

Option A — the diagnosis★ Recommended · currently live

Every business has Weakends. We fix them.

Weakends is a media & AI agency for day-to-day business owners. Content, ads, automation — we take over whatever's weakest in your operation and make it run strong all week.

Why it works — The brand name IS the punchline, unsplit: the visitor reads "Weakends," hears "weak ends," and self-diagnoses in 3 seconds. The orange scribble under the word gives the motion moment.

Option B — the challenge

What's your biggest Weakend?

Content, ads, or the hours you lose to busywork? We find it and fix it — and you don't pay a retainer until the numbers prove we did.

Why it works — Opens with a question the visitor answers in their head, then plugs the performance trial in the very first breath. Strongest if the trial is THE differentiator you want to lead with.

Option C — the promise

Strong all week. Even the Weakends.

Weakends runs the content, ads, and automation your business needs daily — so your operation never takes a day off, even when you do.

Why it works — Doubles the pun (weekends / weak ends) and sells the outcome: a business that runs while you rest. Softest sell of the three; best if you want charm over punch.

05 · Motion plan

Where the page moves (and where it doesn't)

  • Hero: words bounce in one-by-one on load; orange scribble draws itself under "Weakends" — the one orchestrated moment.
  • Marquee: infinite scroll, pauses on hover.
  • Service tabs: smooth accordion, first tab open by default so the pattern reads instantly.
  • Sections: fade-and-rise on scroll (IntersectionObserver — same technique as Clay, no libraries).
  • Hovers: cards lift, wordmark letters jump, CTA nudges up.
  • Restraint rule: nothing else moves. When your reels land in the work grid, they become the main motion and everything around them stays calm.
