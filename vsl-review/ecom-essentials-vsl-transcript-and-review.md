## Part 5 — Scripting review & rating

### Overall: 6.5 / 10 as scripted — solid bones, fixable holes. The structure is textbook VSL; what's missing is numeric consistency, proof specificity, and urgency.

### What's working (keep this)

1. **The structure is genuinely correct.** Hook → failed-attempts callout → empathy ("does this work / is this for me") → credibility intro → unique mechanism (AI + "system, not product") → framework (4 mechanisms) → differentiation (guru contrast + storage-unit proof) → offer stack → objection handling → disqualifying CTA. That's the classic direct-response VSL skeleton and every beat is present.
2. **"It's just me and AI"** is the strongest line in the script and he instinctively repeats it. It's the identity hook, the mechanism, and the objection-killer in five words. The edit should make this the spine.
3. **Specific, relatable failure inventory** — influencer seeding with no content back, booking an Airbnb for a shoot that got no views, hours of content for nothing. Specific beats generic every time; this is his best empathy material.
4. **The storage-unit moment** is real proof-of-work. "I'm currently in my storage unit right now, managing my inventory and packing orders" — that's show-don't-tell, and no guru competitor can copy it.
5. **The "saturated with people guessing" reframe + the mirrors line** is the best-written objection response in the video. "Mirrors have existed for thousands of years, but that didn't stop me from making tens of thousands from mirrors alone" — concrete, memorable, disarming.
6. **The disqualifying CTA** ("store by tonight, ten grand by Friday — I'm not the person") is the right move for a book-a-call funnel. It filters and builds trust simultaneously.
7. **"You don't learn from a course, you learn from somebody who's actually doing what they say they do"** — strong positioning line, especially paired with "documenting the whole journey."

### What's hurting it (fix before/while editing)

1. **The numbers fight each other.** $20K+ since January in the hook vs. "multiple six figures this year" in the intro vs. "$10K products" vs. "multiple tens of thousands." A skeptical viewer catches this instantly, and this avatar is *primed* skeptical. One consistent claim set, ideally the most defensible one, everywhere.
2. **The hook is the weakest beat in the script.** "You could say it's luck, but my brand did $20K" opens by arguing with an accusation nobody made yet, and "that gap is exactly what this video is about" references a gap that was never established. The first 10 seconds are the whole game in a VSL; right now they're the least clear 10 seconds of the video.
3. **No urgency, no risk reversal, no price anchor.** The only scarcity is a vague "before everybody hops on the AI wave." There's no reason to book the call *today*, no guarantee, no cost-of-inaction math, no "spots are limited because I actually run a brand." For a call-booking VSL that's a conversion leak at the exact moment of decision.
4. **Proof is asserted, never shown in the copy.** "Multiple six figures" arrives with no anchor — no screenshot callout, no "here's the Shopify dashboard," no month-by-month. If the visuals will carry receipts, fine — but the script never even points at them ("look at this" doesn't appear once).
5. **The offer stack is generic.** "Direct access, private channel, community, modules, AI systems" is the same stack every e-com program lists. The two differentiated items — *the exact prompts/tools* and *open-book monthly numbers* — are buried at the end of the stack. Lead with what only he can offer.
6. **Objection #3 was never finished** ("you already did most of the hard work. You're already one of the people who...") — as shot, the edit either drops the best objection (previous failures = his exact avatar!) or needs a pickup.
7. **Beat 12 is overwritten** — it took ~11 takes because the sentence is trying to do four jobs. Symptom of the script, not the talent. Split it: "What you're missing is two things: the right information, and the AI systems to execute it. That's the difference between my two years stuck at zero and you doing this in weeks."
8. **The four-mechanism section names get inconsistent** (offer/storefront/engine/operations, but "engine" is described as content twice in overlapping takes, and the first take says "you got the content"). Whatever the final vocab is, the graphics and any recap need to match exactly.
